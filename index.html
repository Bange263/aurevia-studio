Before you purchase

Straight answers.

Understand what Aurevia is, what it includes and what remains specific to your Shopify business.

How do I install Aurevia?

Download and extract the main customer package, then upload Aurevia-Theme-v1.2.1.zip through Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload ZIP file. Do not extract the theme ZIP before uploading it.

Which ZIP file should I upload to Shopify?

Upload only Aurevia-Theme-v1.2.1.zip. The complete customer download contains documentation and other resources, so it is not the file Shopify expects as a theme upload.

Do I need Shopify to use Aurevia?

Yes. Aurevia is a Shopify theme and requires a Shopify store or a compatible Shopify development store where custom theme ZIP files can be uploaded.

Is Aurevia a complete Shopify store?

No. Aurevia is a Shopify theme. Your products, collections, navigation, policies, payments, shipping and apps are configured separately.

Can I customize colors, typography and sections?

Yes. Aurevia includes global theme settings and configurable Shopify sections for normal storefront customization.

Does Aurevia work on mobile?

Yes. Navigation, search, product pages, cart and content sections are responsive.

Are the VELORA LABS products included as real products?

No. VELORA LABS and all cosmetics, prices and testimonials are fictional demonstration content. A portable demo CSV and imagery are included for setup purposes.

Can I use one purchase for several stores?

No. The standard license covers one commercial Shopify store. Contact support for a separate multi-store or agency arrangement.

Are Shopify or third-party app fees included?

No. Shopify plans, domains, paid apps, fonts, photography and external services are separate.

What support is included?

Support covers installation guidance and confirmed Aurevia theme defects. Store setup, custom development and third-party app troubleshooting are not included.

How is Aurevia delivered?

After successful payment through Payhip, the digital download is made available to the customer and a purchase email is sent by the checkout platform.
