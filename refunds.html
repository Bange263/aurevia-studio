Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Aurevia is a digital product delivered electronically. Refund handling is coordinated through the checkout provider and remains subject to mandatory consumer law.

1. Before purchasing

Please review the product description, requirements, previews and FAQ before ordering. Aurevia requires a Shopify store and does not include Shopify subscription fees, a domain, store content, custom development or third-party apps.

2. Digital delivery

The download becomes available after successful payment. Where the checkout asks for express consent to immediate digital delivery and acknowledgement regarding withdrawal rights, those choices affect the statutory cancellation rights available after download begins.

3. Refund requests

Contact if425785@gmail.com promptly with the order email, order number and a clear description of the issue. Requests are assessed fairly under the checkout provider’s procedures and applicable law.

4. Confirmed defects

If the unmodified theme contains a reproducible defect that materially prevents an advertised core function, support will first attempt to reproduce and correct it. A refund may be considered when the confirmed defect cannot be resolved within a reasonable period.

5. Situations normally not eligible

Subject to mandatory law, refunds are normally not granted solely because of a change of mind after download, lack of Shopify knowledge, absence of products/content, third-party app conflicts, custom-code issues, hosting/account problems, or a feature that was not advertised as included.

6. Duplicate purchases and payment errors

Duplicate charges or demonstrable payment errors should be reported immediately and will be reviewed through the checkout provider.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact support before starting a chargeback so the issue can be investigated. Fraudulent or abusive disputes may result in licence termination, without limiting mandatory rights.
